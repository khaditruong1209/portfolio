Project Overview

This freelance project focused on producing AI-generated visual assets for e-commerce and digital marketing. Working from client briefs, I transformed product requirements and creative references into marketing visuals suitable for websites, advertisements, social media, and promotional campaigns. Rather than simply generating images, the process emphasized understanding brand identity, ensuring visual consistency, and refining outputs until they met commercial quality standards.
