Project Overview

The website serves as the primary touchpoint for customer acquisition and online sales at WIGI Hair. My role focused on continuously improving website usability, navigation, content presentation, and conversion opportunities based on customer behavior and business objectives.

Using website analytics and user behavior insights, I collaborated on optimization initiatives that enhanced the customer journey across desktop and mobile experiences.
