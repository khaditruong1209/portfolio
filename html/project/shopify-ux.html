Project Overview

As a Marketing Executive at GEW Vietnam, I focused on optimizing the Shopify experience for WIGI Hair by improving website usability, navigation, and conversion opportunities. My work included customizing Shopify sections, refining website structure, enhancing product discovery, analyzing user behavior with analytics tools, and implementing improvements that supported a smoother customer journey.

My Responsibilities

01. Shopify Website Optimization

Customized and optimized Shopify pages, sections, and theme components to improve usability, responsiveness, and overall website performance.

02. Navigation & User Journey

Improved website navigation, menu structure, collection organization, and customer journeys to help visitors discover products more efficiently.

03. UX & Conversion Optimization

Identified usability issues and implemented interface improvements to enhance customer experience and support higher conversion opportunities.

04. Analytics & User Behavior

Monitored customer behavior using analytics tools such as Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to evaluate website performance and identify optimization opportunities.

Optimization Process

01. Analyze

Reviewed analytics, heatmaps, and user recordings to identify UX issues.

02. Research

03. Improve

04. Measure

Monitored website performance after each improvement to support continuous optimization.
