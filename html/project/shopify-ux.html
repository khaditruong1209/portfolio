Shopify & UX Optimization

Optimizing Shopify storefronts through UX improvements, customer behavior analysis, SEO optimization, and conversion-focused strategies to create a better online shopping experience.

About WIGI Hair

WIGI Hair is a premium e-commerce brand specializing in high-quality human hair extensions for customers in the United States. Built on Shopify, the brand focuses on delivering a seamless online shopping experience through intuitive website navigation, engaging product pages, and customer-centered design.

Project Overview

Besides managing the Shopify store, I continuously analyzed customer behavior and website performance to identify opportunities for UX improvements. My work covered product pages, collection pages, navigation, search functionality, SEO optimization, and overall conversion rate improvements.

My Responsibilities

Shopify Store Management

Managed products, collections, navigation, menus, pages, and store configurations.

UX & UI Optimization

Improved website usability, homepage layouts, product pages, and collection experiences to create smoother customer journeys.

Performance Analysis

Used Shopify Analytics, Google Analytics 4, and Microsoft Clarity to understand customer behavior and identify optimization opportunities.

SEO Enhancement

Optimized page titles, meta descriptions, internal links, and content structures to improve search performance.

Optimization Process

01. Analyze

Reviewed analytics, heatmaps, and user recordings to identify UX issues.

02. Research

Compared competitors, reviewed best practices, and evaluated customer feedback.

03. Improve

Updated layouts, product pages, navigation, and content to improve usability.

04. Measure

Monitored website performance after each improvement to support continuous optimization.
