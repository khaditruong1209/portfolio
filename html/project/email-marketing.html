Project Overview

As a Marketing Executive at GEW Vietnam, I managed email marketing activities for WIGI Hair using Klaviyo. My responsibilities included planning promotional campaigns, building automated email flows, segmenting customer audiences, coordinating email content and visuals, and analyzing campaign performance to improve customer engagement and retention.

My Responsibilities

01. Campaign Planning & Execution

Planned and executed promotional email campaigns for seasonal sales, product launches, and marketing events while maintaining brand consistency across all communications.

02. Marketing Automation

Built, maintained, and optimized automated email flows, including Welcome Series, Abandoned Cart, Browse Abandonment, and post-purchase journeys to improve customer engagement.

03. Audience Segmentation & Content Management

Managed customer segments, coordinated email content and visuals, and ensured campaigns reached the right audience with relevant messaging.

04. Performance Analysis & Optimization

Monitored campaign performance through key metrics such as open rate, click rate, and conversion rate, using insights to continuously optimize future campaigns.
