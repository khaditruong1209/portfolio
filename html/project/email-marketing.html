Project Overview

Email marketing was an important part of WIGI Hair's integrated marketing strategy. Rather than sending promotional emails only, I planned lifecycle campaigns based on customer behavior, purchase stages, and marketing objectives. Working alongside website optimization and paid media, email marketing helped improve customer engagement, retention, and repeat purchase opportunities.

My role included campaign planning, customer segmentation, automation management, performance monitoring, and continuous optimization using campaign data.

Email Campaign Planning

Audience Segmentation

Grouped customers based on behavior, purchase history, and engagement level.

Campaign Planning

Planned promotional, seasonal, educational, and retention campaigns aligned with marketing objectives.

Automation Strategy

Designed customer journeys including Welcome Series, Browse Abandonment, Abandoned Cart, and Post-purchase flows.

Performance Review

Monitored campaign performance and optimized content, timing, and audience targeting.
