About TIEM NHA CO

TIEM NHA CO is a family-owned online secondhand fashion business founded by my family and me. We specialize in carefully selected pre-owned clothing, selling exclusively through Facebook and Instagram. The business focuses on making secondhand fashion more accessible while building a trustworthy and engaging shopping experience for our online community.

Project Overview

As the person responsible for marketing, I designed promotional visuals, created product content, and managed Meta advertising campaigns. My work focused on presenting products professionally, attracting potential customers, and increasing online sales through creative design and performance-driven advertising.

My Responsibilities

Graphic Design

Designed promotional banners, product layouts, social media posts, and campaign visuals to build a consistent visual identity.

Product Content

Prepared product images, edited photos, and created engaging captions for Facebook and Instagram.

Meta Advertising

Created and managed Meta advertising campaigns, including audience targeting, budget planning, and campaign optimization.

Marketing Planning

Planned promotional activities, seasonal campaigns, and collaborated with family members to support business growth.

Campaign Workflow

01. Product Selection

Select secondhand products, categorize collections, and prepare items before launching campaigns.

02. Creative Design

Design product visuals, promotional banners, and social media creatives that reflect the brand identity.

03. Launch Campaign

Publish content, launch Meta advertising campaigns, and promote new collections through Facebook and Instagram.

04. Optimize

Monitor campaign performance, adjust creatives, refine audience targeting, and improve advertising results.
