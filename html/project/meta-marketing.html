About TIEM NHA CO

TIEM NHA CO is a local café brand focusing on creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere for customers. The marketing strategy centered on strengthening brand awareness, engaging local audiences, and driving in-store visits through social media content and paid advertising.

Project Overview

I was responsible for managing Facebook and Instagram, planning monthly content calendars, creating promotional campaigns, and supporting Meta advertising. My work focused on increasing audience engagement while maintaining a consistent visual identity across all social media channels.

My Responsibilities

Content Planning

Developed monthly content calendars aligned with seasonal promotions and business objectives.

Social Media Management

Managed Facebook and Instagram, scheduled posts, and interacted with customers.

Meta Ads

Supported campaign setup, budget allocation, and advertising optimization through Meta Ads Manager.

Performance Analysis

Monitored reach, engagement, and campaign performance to improve future content strategies.
