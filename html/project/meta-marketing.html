Project Overview

This project focused on managing Meta marketing activities for an online fashion brand. My responsibilities covered campaign planning, content strategy, community engagement, paid advertising, and performance monitoring to strengthen brand visibility and support online sales. Rather than focusing solely on ad execution, the project emphasized understanding customer interests, maintaining a consistent brand presence, and continuously improving campaign effectiveness through performance insights.
