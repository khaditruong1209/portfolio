Project Overview

As the Co-founder and Marketing Executive of Tiệm Nhà Cỏ, I managed the brand's Facebook and Instagram presence while supporting business growth through Meta advertising. My responsibilities included planning social media content, publishing posts, managing Meta Ads campaigns, monitoring campaign performance, and maintaining a consistent brand presence across social media platforms.
