Created social media content, promotional campaigns, product photography, and managed Meta advertising for TIEM NHA CO — a secondhand fashion startup. My work focused on building a consistent brand identity, increasing customer engagement, and supporting online sales across Facebook and Instagram.

About TIEM NHA CO

TIEM NHA CO is a small online secondhand fashion startup co-founded by a family member and me. We carefully curate quality pre-owned clothing and sell exclusively through Facebook and Instagram. Our mission is to make secondhand fashion more accessible while creating a trustworthy, enjoyable, and community-driven shopping experience.

Project Overview

As the co-founder and marketing lead of TIEM NHA CO, I was responsible for building the brand's online presence through Facebook and Instagram. My work covered the entire content creation process—from planning campaigns, photographing products, designing promotional visuals, creating short-form videos, writing engaging captions, to managing Meta advertising campaigns. Every piece of content was designed to strengthen brand identity, increase customer engagement, and support online sales.

My Responsibilities

Content Planning

Planned monthly content calendars, promotional campaigns, and seasonal marketing activities for Facebook and Instagram.

Product Photography

Styled, photographed, and edited product images to create visually appealing social media content.

Creative Design

Designed promotional banners, social media posts, story templates, and campaign visuals while maintaining a consistent brand identity.

Video Production

Filmed and edited short-form videos, including product showcases, behind-the-scenes content, and promotional Reels for Facebook and Instagram.

Social Media Management

Managed Facebook and Instagram pages, published content, engaged with customers, and maintained a consistent posting schedule.

Meta Advertising

Created and optimized Meta advertising campaigns, including audience targeting, budget allocation, creative testing, and campaign monitoring.
