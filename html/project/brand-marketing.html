About WIGI Hair

WIGI Hair is an e-commerce brand specializing in premium human hair extensions, helping women achieve natural, confidence-boosting hairstyles. The brand primarily serves customers across the United States and focuses on delivering high-quality products, personalized customer support, and a seamless online shopping experience.

Project Overview

As a Marketing Executive, I supported the development of WIGI Hair's brand through daily marketing activities. My work included coordinating creative assets, supporting promotional campaigns, maintaining brand consistency, and collaborating with designers to ensure a unified customer experience across the website, email marketing, and other digital channels.

Project Objective

Strengthen WIGI Hair's brand presence by delivering consistent visual communication and supporting integrated marketing campaigns across multiple customer touchpoints.
