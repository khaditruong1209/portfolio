Project Overview

As a Marketing Executive, I managed WIGI Hair's Shopify website and supported the brand's organic growth through SEO. My responsibilities included maintaining website content, creating promotional landing pages, publishing and reviewing blog articles, optimizing on-page SEO, and monitoring search performance to improve visibility and user experience.

My Responsibilities

01. Shopify Website Management

Maintained and updated the Shopify storefront by managing homepage banners, collection pages, product pages, navigation menus, footer sections, and other website components to ensure a consistent and engaging shopping experience.

02. Landing Page Development

Built and maintained landing pages for seasonal campaigns, product launches, and promotional events while ensuring consistency with the brand identity and marketing objectives.

03. SEO Content Management

Published and reviewed blog content, optimized page metadata and keywords, submitted pages for indexing, and maintained on-page SEO to improve search engine visibility.

04. SEO Performance Monitoring

Monitored keyword performance, indexing status, and website search visibility using SEO tools while preparing reports to support continuous optimization.
