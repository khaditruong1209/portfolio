Business Challenge

As WIGI Hair expanded into the U.S. market, the brand needed to strengthen its market presence while maintaining a consistent customer experience across multiple digital touchpoints. Although marketing activities were already running across social media, advertising, email, and e-commerce, these channels required better alignment to improve campaign effectiveness, customer engagement, and long-term brand growth.

Objectives

Increase brand awareness in the U.S. market.

Support product launches and seasonal campaigns.

Improve consistency across marketing channels.

Enhance customer engagement throughout the buying journey.

Drive qualified traffic to the Shopify website.

Research & Insights

Research Sources

Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity, Shopify Analytics, Meta Ads Manager, Klaviyo Reports, Competitor Analysis, Customer Feedback

Key Insights

Educational and lifestyle-focused content generated stronger engagement than promotional-only content. Customers interacted with multiple touchpoints before purchasing. Website experience and email marketing significantly influenced customer retention.

Marketing Strategy

Based on research findings, I supported the development of an integrated marketing strategy focused on creating a consistent customer experience across digital channels.
