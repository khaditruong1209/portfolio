Business Challenge

As WIGI Hair expanded into the U.S. market, the brand needed to strengthen its market presence while maintaining a consistent customer experience across multiple digital touchpoints. Although marketing activities were already running across social media, advertising, email, and e-commerce, these channels required better alignment to improve campaign effectiveness, customer engagement, and long-term brand growth.

Objectives

Increase brand awareness in the U.S. market.

Support product launches and seasonal campaigns.

Improve consistency across marketing channels.

Enhance customer engagement throughout the buying journey.

Drive qualified traffic to the Shopify website.

Key Insights

Customer Behavior

Customers responded more positively to educational content, styling tips, and authentic product demonstrations than to purely promotional posts.

Channel Performance

Marketing campaigns achieved stronger engagement when social media, email marketing, and website content delivered consistent messaging.

Website Experience

Improving navigation, product information, and landing page structure helped create a smoother shopping experience.

Marketing Strategy

Based on the research findings, I supported an integrated marketing approach focusing on four key priorities:

Integrated Campaign Planning

Coordinated promotional campaigns across social media, website, email marketing, and paid advertising to ensure consistent customer communication.

Content & Brand Consistency

Maintained unified messaging and visual identity across digital touchpoints to strengthen brand recognition.

Customer Journey Optimization

Improved the customer experience by aligning website content, landing pages, and email marketing with campaign objectives.

Performance Monitoring

Tracked campaign performance through analytics and customer behavior data to continuously identify optimization opportunities.
