About Delta Engagement Marketing

Delta Engagement Marketing is a Below-the-Line (BTL) marketing agency specializing in brand activations, experiential marketing, retail campaigns, and events. Working alongside planners and account teams, I assisted in transforming client briefs into activation concepts and proposal presentations that aligned with business objectives and consumer insights.

02. Research

03. Creative Concept

Develop activation ideas, campaign mechanics, and event experiences that match the brand direction.

04. Presentation Proposal

Compile concepts into proposal decks including timelines, activation flow, and presentation materials.
