Project Overview

Rather than executing marketing campaigns, this internship focused on supporting the planning phase of brand activation projects. I collaborated with planners and account teams to research markets, analyze consumer behavior, develop campaign concepts, and prepare client proposals across multiple industries.

My Contributions

Consumer & Market Research

Collected market information, analyzed competitors, and identified consumer trends to support campaign planning.

Campaign Concept Development

Supported brainstorming sessions and translated research findings into activation ideas aligned with client objectives.

Proposal Development

Prepared presentation materials that clearly communicated campaign strategy, activation concepts, and execution recommendations.
